    Seat layouts

    Pick a screen, shape its seats, aisles and screen position, then save. Row A is always the row nearest the screen.

    1. Screen details
    2. Grid size
    3. Paint seats

    Pick a seat category (or Aisle / gap), then click or drag across the grid. Click a row letter to paint the whole row. Seats are numbered automatically and gaps are skipped.

    Saved

    Saved layouts apply to new shows. Shows already scheduled keep the layout they were created with, so booked seats never move.
