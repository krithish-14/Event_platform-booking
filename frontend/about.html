Our Story

From Chennai, with ambition and a love for great events.

JOD Events started with a simple idea: that well-planned events shouldn't be reserved for only the largest budgets or biggest brands. Every occasion - whether it's an annual company day, a product launch, a wedding, or a community festival - deserves the same level of care, creativity and attention to detail.

Headquartered in Chennai, we've grown from a small team of passionate event planners into a full-service production house trusted by corporates, institutions, families and cultural organizations across South India. Along the way, we've delivered 500+ events, worked across 12+ industries, and hosted crowds of up to 5,000 guests per occasion.

Our goal has never been to be the biggest - just the most reliable partner you can think of when an experience truly matters.
