Our Story

From a Creative Beginning to a Growing Event Brand.

JOD’s story began in Chennai with JOD Studios in 2017, built around creativity, production, and bringing ideas to life.

In May 2019, JOD Events was born. What started as a passion for creating memorable experiences grew into an event brand delivering corporate events, weddings, launches, celebrations, exhibitions, and more.

In 2024, JOD Business Services became the next chapter in the story, expanding the JOD ecosystem beyond events into business, digital, branding, and growth.

Today, every chapter connects back to the same belief: great ideas deserve great execution.
