Host & Ticketing Agreement

Electronic Acceptance Version

Effective date: Today  |  Agreement version: JOD-HTA-1.0

Between JOD Business Services, operating JOD Events Portal (“JOD”), and the individual or entity identified in the verified Host registration record (“Host”). The Host’s verified legal name, address, PAN/GSTIN (where applicable), authorised signatory and contact email form part of this Agreement.

1. Services and Roles

JOD provides event listing, booking, ticketing and payment facilitation as a technology intermediary. The Host is the supplier and organiser of each event and is responsible for its performance, advertised claims, attendee experience and statutory obligations. JOD does not become the event organiser merely by listing or ticketing the event.

2. Introductory Platform Fee

JOD charges 5% of the ticket sale value excluding GST on the underlying event, PLUS applicable GST on JOD’s commission (currently 18%). The 5% includes standard domestic payment gateway processing costs. Any special marketing, international processing, premium payment methods or other optional fees require prior written disclosure and Host acceptance. The agreed introductory rate applies to confirmed events; future changes require advance notice and acceptance.

3. Event Prices and Taxes

The Host determines ticket prices and declares the correct event tax treatment, including applicable GST rate, exemption and registration status. Where law places GST on the event supply on the Host, the Host accounts for and remits it. JOD accounts for GST on JOD’s own commission and complies with any withholding, collection, reporting or other obligations directly imposed on JOD by law. The Host shall supply accurate tax and invoice details.

4. Payment Collection and Settlement

Customer payments are processed through JOD’s integrated payment gateway. JOD shall initiate payment of net eligible ticket proceeds within T+5 working days for domestic payments and T+12 working days for international payments, where T is the date of successful ticket payment and capture. Working days exclude applicable bank holidays and days on which relevant banking settlement services are unavailable. Actual bank credit may take additional processing time. Net proceeds equal gross ticket collections less JOD commission, GST on that commission, deductions required by law, refunds, chargebacks and expressly agreed adjustments. Payment is subject to successful KYC, verified bank details, payment-provider rules, fraud checks and lawful holds. JOD shall provide an itemised settlement statement.

5. Mandatory Host KYC

Full KYC verification and JOD approval are mandatory before paid events can be published or payouts enabled. Depending on Host type, JOD may require legal name, verified email and phone, PAN, address and identity evidence, business registration/constitution documents, GSTIN or lawful non-registration declaration, beneficial ownership/authorised signatory evidence, and bank account verification. Document submission alone is not approval. JOD may request updated information, reverify, reject or suspend an account where legally or operationally justified.

6. Event Category, Licences and Safety

For every event, the Host must disclose category, activities, venue, date, capacity, age restrictions, pricing, cancellation policy and relevant tax information. The Host is responsible for permits, licences, music/public performance rights where applicable, venue permissions, insurance where required, attendee safety, consumer-law compliance and any special permissions for regulated or higher-risk events. JOD may require proof before approval.

7. Restricted Events and Changes

JOD may decline or suspend unlawful, misleading, fraudulent, unsafe, unlicensed or payment-provider-prohibited events. Alcohol-related, age-restricted, adventure, high-risk or other regulated experiences may undergo enhanced review. The Host must promptly disclose material changes in event scope, date, venue, ownership, tax status or bank details; material event changes may require fresh approval.

8. Cancellations, Refunds and Disputes

The Host must publish a lawful refund policy before sales. The Host bears refunds arising from Host cancellation, material change or non-delivery to the extent required by law. JOD may process eligible refunds and adjust unpaid balances or seek reimbursement for already settled amounts. Chargeback and unrecoverable processing costs may be charged to the Host only as permitted by law and disclosed terms. Parties shall cooperate on disputes.

9. Promotional Materials and Data

The Host grants JOD permission to display event information, brand assets and supplied creatives to list and promote the event, and warrants rights to those materials. JOD will handle KYC and personal data under its privacy notice and applicable data protection law, using authorised verification and payment providers where necessary. Host must not upload unnecessary sensitive personal data.

10. Email OTP Verification and Electronic Acceptance

The Host must first verify control of the registered email address using a one-time password (OTP). After viewing the complete agreement, the Host must separately and affirmatively select “I have read and agree to the JOD Host & Ticketing Agreement” and click “Accept Agreement”. The person accepting represents that they are the Host or have authority to bind the Host. JOD records the exact agreement version, acceptance text, Host ID, verified email, OTP verification event, UTC timestamp, IP address and relevant audit metadata, and provides a downloadable acceptance record/PDF. Email OTP is an authentication step and is not represented as a certified digital signature. The parties intend electronic acceptance to form a binding contract to the extent permitted by applicable law.

11. Suspension, Liability and Term

JOD may restrict new listings or settlements for incomplete KYC, suspected fraud, legal obligations or material breach, subject to applicable law and payment-provider requirements. Each party remains responsible for its own breach, negligence and statutory obligations. Either party may terminate with 15 days’ written notice; existing bookings, refunds, settlements and accrued liabilities survive termination.

12. Governing Law

Indian law governs this Agreement. Subject to applicable law, competent courts in Chennai, Tamil Nadu shall have jurisdiction.
