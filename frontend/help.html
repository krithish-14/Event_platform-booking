Frequently Asked Questions

How do I access my event E-Tickets? +
Once your booking is confirmed, your E-Tickets are available directly under Your Orders in your profile dropdown. You will also receive an email with the PDF ticket attachment and QR code.
What is the event cancellation and refund policy? +
Cancellations made 48 hours prior to the event start time are eligible for a 100% refund. Refunds are processed back to your original payment method within 3–5 business days.
Can I transfer my ticket to another attendee? +
Yes! You can transfer your ticket by navigating to Your Orders, selecting the ticket, and updating the attendee name and email.
How do I change or upload my profile picture? +
Click on your profile avatar icon in the top header and select Dashboard or Settings. Click on your avatar circle to upload your photo.
What should I bring for check-in at the venue? +
Please present your digital E-Ticket QR code on your smartphone or a printed copy along with a valid government ID matching the primary ticket holder's name.
