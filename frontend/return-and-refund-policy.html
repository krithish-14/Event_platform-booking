Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: August 2026. Clear, transparent guidelines on ticket cancellations, pass transfers, and refund processing.

1. Ticket Cancellation Window

We understand that schedules change. Depending on when you request a cancellation before an event, the following refund rules apply:

  • 7+ Days Before Event: 100% full refund (minus payment processing gateway fee).
  • 3 to 6 Days Before Event: 50% partial refund or 100% event credit voucher valid for 12 months.
  • Under 48 Hours Before Event: Non-refundable due to venue commitments, catering, and seat reservations.

2. Event Cancellation by Organizer

If an event is cancelled by JOD Events or the event organizer due to weather, safety concerns, or unavoidable emergencies, all registered attendees will receive a 100% automatic refund to their original payment method within 5–7 business days.

3. Ticket Transfer & Substitution

Can't make it? You can transfer your pass or ticket to a colleague or friend free of charge up to 12 hours before event start time via your Orders Dashboard or by contacting customer support.

4. How Refund Processing Works

Approved refunds are credited back to the original bank account, credit/debit card, or UPI ID used during checkout. Bank processing typically takes 3 to 7 business days depending on your financial institution.

5. Requesting a Refund

To request a refund or cancellation, please visit your Orders Page or submit a request to support@jodevents.com with your Order ID (#JOD-XXXXX).
