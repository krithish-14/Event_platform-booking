Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026. Your privacy and data security are fundamental to how JOD Events operates.

1. Information We Collect

When you use JOD Events (register for events, purchase tickets, subscribe to updates, or request consultation), we collect personal information you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Account & Profile Data: Full name, email address, username, phone number, and optional profile picture.
  • Booking & Transaction Details: Event registrations, ticket types, attendance history, and payment status (processed via PCI-compliant payment gateways).
  • Communication Records: Messages, feedback, and support inquiry logs.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your personal data strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Issuing digital tickets, booking confirmations, and event reminders.
  • Providing personalized event recommendations based on your preferences and location.
  • Improving site performance, security, and user experience.
  • Communicating essential event updates or venue changes.

3. Data Protection & Security

We employ industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS) and secure database access controls. Password hashes are encrypted using bcrypt algorithms with salt protection. We do not store raw credit card details on our servers.

4. Third-Party Sharing

JOD Events does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Data is shared only with verified service partners (such as payment processors and venue security coordinators) solely to execute event logistics.

5. Your Rights & Choices

You have the right to access, update, or delete your account information at any time via your account settings page. You may also opt out of promotional communications at any time.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please email our privacy team at privacy@jodevents.com.
